# The Last Platform multi-character conformance campaign

This campaign proves the declarative system can derive reusable geometry and motion from verified parents with explicit lineage rather than modifying library files, copying geometry, or hand-scaling animation curves. The platform adaptation adds dialogue marks and camera anchors, preserves topology/skeleton/coordinates, and emits a hashed compatibility report. The motion adaptation re-synthesizes the verified cautious phase gait against Elara's declared proportions and repeats canonical-skeleton, forward-direction, contact, clearance, penetration, continuity, and joint-limit checks. Two different verified characters are placed from semantic marks, receive independent head-performance clips, and must pass quantitative mutual-facing plus per-subject framing/exposure gates.

Both adaptations declare immutable publication candidates. The environment package requires `environment.night-transit-platform@1.0.1`; the current motion package requires `motion.walk-natural-cautious@1.1.1` and `character.elara-vale@0.1.4`. Each includes its compatibility report, exact campaign source, and rendered evidence. The current releases are `environment.night-transit-dialogue-stage@1.0.1` and `motion.walk-natural-cautious-elara@1.1.0`; earlier immutable releases remain available to campaigns that explicitly require them.

The accepted delivery is 640×360, 24 fps, 216 frames, and exactly 9.000 seconds with H.264/yuv420p video and AAC stereo 48 kHz audio. Its soundtrack now resolves `audio.beacon-one-product-pulse@1.0.0`, originally derived and approved in the unrelated Beacon One product campaign, with `reusedAudioSources: 1`, `adaptedAudioSources: 0`, and no copied filter graph. Every shot records 2.3 m character separation with 1.0 facing alignment in both directions. Wide shots require complete characters; the two over-shoulder shots instead enforce minimum visible projected area and screen height for the foreground actor while retaining full framing for the listener.
