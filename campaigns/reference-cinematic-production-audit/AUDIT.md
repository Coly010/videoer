# Reference cinematic production audit

Status: audit concluded on evidence saturation; visual acceptance is **failed**.

This campaign deliberately recombines the strongest currently accepted reusable assets after the firelit-lighting milestone. It is an audit fixture, not a second bespoke benchmark implementation. Its purpose is to reveal which shared subsystem now produces the largest visible distance from the supplied reference class.

The benchmark is a periodic system audit, not the primary development target. Complete this integration pass, preserve its rejected evidence, and use the resulting ranking to improve reusable character, environment, material, lighting, VFX, audio, and production infrastructure. Do not iterate on benchmark-specific staging, coordinates, meshes, or effects unless they expose and exercise a defect in a shared abstraction. Rerun the benchmark after a material tranche of reusable-system improvement so it measures transfer and identifies the next largest gap; do not continuously tune the benchmark between those milestones.

Every subsystem follow-up must begin with the production-wide ADR 023 research survey: current permissively licensed OSS implementations/assets, open datasets, papers, technical blogs, community wikis, standards, and authoritative engine/DCC documentation. This applies to rigs and characters as well as geometry, environments, textures, materials, lighting, render settings, simulation, hair/fluid/particles, VFX, sound, compositing and verification. Reuse legally and technically suitable work through replaceable boundaries, or carry its documented techniques and data into project-owned code when direct reuse is inappropriate. Record provenance, licence, version/date, compatibility and the precise decision so speed does not come at the cost of hidden dependency or licensing risk.

## Acceptance standard

The environment must not pass because it is readable, coherent, technically valid, or intentionally stylised. At the intended vertical-trailer camera distances it must approach photographic material response, geometric density, atmospheric integration, and believable construction. Until an informed viewer would no longer describe it as a clean procedural blockout or low-detail game set, photorealistic environment quality remains failed.

## Evidence

The full-quality sequence render was deliberately stopped after four completed authoritative shots and the reach/grasp landmarks of the fifth. By that point each additional character frame was repeating the same proxy, deformation, composition and environment failures, while the audit had already exposed new generic defects in verification aggregation, character composition contracts and assembled-scene interaction validation. Earlier authoritative probes retain the book interaction and final editorial evidence. Continuing the remaining frames would spend substantial deterministic Cycles compute without a plausible path to changing the subsystem ranking. This is an evidence-saturation decision, not a visual pass, a reduced-quality substitute or permission to ship the incomplete edit. The interrupted reach/grasp observations are retained under `verification/observations/enter-bookshop/`.

The completed exterior establishing probe improves cool/warm separation, wet-surface reflections, deterministic rain, and reusable prop dressing. It still fails the target class because:

- façades are flat monolithic planes with little construction logic or depth;
- windows read as oversized uniform emissive slabs rather than glazed openings with frames, rooms, curtains, occlusion, and varied occupancy;
- rooflines, wall edges, sills, thresholds, drainage, paving boundaries, and joins lack believable profiles and wear;
- cobbles repeat too evenly and reflect as a regular tiled surface rather than irregular wet stone with puddling, dirt, chips, and local roughness variation;
- object density and semantic street dressing remain sparse;
- rain reads as a foreground overlay more than droplets integrated with depth, wind, splashes, runoff, mist, and practical light;
- large unbroken black/blue masses suppress atmospheric depth and material identity.

The earlier character and book-interaction probes also remain rejected: the published Elara proxy has low-detail face, hands, hair, garment silhouette, and deformation, while the newer full MakeHuman/Rigify boundary is not yet a clothed, hair-bound, identity-finished production character.

The authoritative character-approach render confirms this under final lighting rather than only in a cheap probe. The stronger warm-window/cool-exterior separation does not conceal the proxy: the profile face and torso are visibly faceted, the hand is an undifferentiated mitten/wedge, the pulled-back hair reads as a hard helmet mass, the sleeve and dress lack cloth construction or fine folds, and the body/garment silhouette has little anatomical loading. The adjacent window is still a shallow glowing recess with oversized panes, while paving courses end in clean floating-looking seams. This shot therefore fails character representation, deformation/performance, cloth/hair, and environment construction quality simultaneously.

Its authoritative report also fails the declared visibility gate: the four semantic frames measure 75%, 76%, 61%, and 56% black against a 40% maximum. The renderer reproduces the declarative camera exactly and the character travels 1.337 m with a 0.99994 forward-facing dot, so this is not another backwards-walk orientation defect. It is an exposure/occlusion/composition failure layered on top of the visible asset failures. The failed report and preview are preserved under `verification/shots/character-approach/`; encoding success is not acceptance.

The completed `window-attraction` shot passes its renderer camera contract and body/travel alignment gate (0.7216 m travel, forward dot 1.0), but the authoritative intended-camera contact sheet is visually rejected. Across all four semantic frames, the shoulder and sleeve remain an over-inflated faceted cylinder, the elbow transition pinches mechanically, the hand is a tapered block without usable anatomy, and the low-poly face dominates the close crop. The window behind her remains a dark shallow panel with little glazed/interior depth. The intended gaze/turn action is readable, but that functional fact does not offset failed character surface, deformation, framing, exposure, and architectural construction. Evidence is preserved under `verification/shots/window-attraction/`.

The completed `window-pause` shot is also visually rejected. Holding the root removes locomotion as a confounder but leaves the same over-inflated horizontal shoulder/sleeve, segmented elbow, capsule hand, faceted face and helmet-like hair. The four-frame composition remains an extreme torso crop against a mostly black shallow window, so neither the torso-led turn nor gaze settlement reads clearly. Its only declared renderer check is camera-contract fidelity and passes; this is direct evidence that camera execution alone cannot stand in for composition or action readability. Evidence is preserved under `verification/shots/window-pause/`.

The live `enter-bookshop` audit initially made the reaching arm appear screen-left, but a canonical joint/transform trace proves there is no handedness swap: the anatomical right chain performs the reach after the actor's π yaw. At authoritative frame 16 the right grip is 7.72 mm from the handle while the left is 0.997 m away; frame 17 establishes effectively exact contact. The visual failure is therefore poor readability and mitten-like grasp representation, not incorrect side mapping. The trace exposed a more important generic gap: asset synthesis verifies contact, but the assembled cinematic scene carries actor and target as unrelated entity/motion bindings and never revalidates hand ownership/contact after transforms or source-time remapping. The scene also ignores the environment's named `door-approach` anchor, contributing to placement risk.

The reusable interaction follow-up is explicit: enforce declared hand-to-effector consistency in the interaction schema; add a scene-level interaction/contact gate referencing actor effector, target attachment, active phase interval, transforms, clips and remapped source time; verify host-anchor alignment and ground support; and add Blender inspect-only basis-parity tests. Swapped effectors, post-synthesis actor/target transforms and mismatched time remapping must all fail. This is not a reason to alter this benchmark's hand or door coordinates.

Independent visual QA also exposed an acceptance-aggregation defect: the original character-approach report kept structural `verification.status: pass` even though its renderer visibility check failed, leaving a misleading top-level status despite the render operation throwing. The shared cinematic renderer now aggregates structural and renderer checks into one report verification result while retaining `renderChecks` as explicit renderer evidence. A unit regression proves that any failed renderer check makes the aggregate fail. Existing pixels were refreshed through Blender inspect-only mode; the promoted character-approach report now correctly records aggregate `fail` without rerendering or weakening its evidence.

The audit also records verification coverage gaps rather than treating the fixed aggregation as sufficient. Camera-contract fidelity proves execution of declared transforms, not usable composition. Current framing evidence can record extreme cropped/off-frame projections without a mandatory failure, and direction-of-travel checks do not prove visible foot contact, knee/pose orientation, gaze readability, limb/cloth/hair deformation, temporal smoothness, material realism, atmospheric depth or subject salience. These require reusable fail-closed gates and visual review at the relevant shot distance.

The next composition-verification tranche is now specified generically. Every visible character must declare either `subject-framing` for a fully framed composition or `subject-coverage` for an intentional crop/close-up; inventory-oriented `entity-set-*` gates cannot substitute. Partial-subject coverage must add an explicit maximum raw projected-height contract, and every semantic landmark must pass rather than accepting one useful frame or an average. Blender framing evidence must distinguish fully projectable, behind-camera, near-plane-intersecting, beyond-far-plane and empty geometry while retaining raw projected and viewport-clipped bounds. Near-plane or behind-camera subject geometry fails. This would reject the undeclared 142–215% Elara projections in `window-attraction` while still allowing a deliberately bounded close-up. It proves geometric composition only; semantic action readability remains a separate visual/body-region gate.

## Environment root cause

Inspection of `src/environments/bookshop.ts` confirms that this is not primarily a colour-grade problem. The opposing street and neighbouring buildings are predominantly large axis-aligned `boxPart` masses; many opposite windows are shallow emissive boxes; and the cobbles are repeated rectangular boxes with bounded size/height jitter. Procedural materials add useful colour, normal, roughness, and wetness variation, but cannot supply absent silhouette or construction information. The next environment tranche therefore needs a reusable architectural-detail grammar with varied roof/eave profiles, bevelled and damaged edges, true recessed openings, complete window assemblies and room occupancy, masonry/plaster layer boundaries, thresholds/drainage/runoff, irregular paving borders and settlement, and density rules for semantic street dressing. Texture enrichment alone would leave the principal failure intact.

A code-grounded transfer analysis makes that next tranche concrete: add a renderer-independent construction-aware architectural-envelope grammar and paired irregular-paving grammar, extending the existing real-aperture, surface-query, portable-window, canopy, rainwater, sign, lantern and dressing contracts rather than replacing them. The envelope should declare footprint/floors/bay rhythm, wall thickness, actual apertures, façade layer stacks, bevel/chip treatment, roof/eave/cornice profiles, thresholds/foundations, window room depth/occupancy, semantic attachment anchors and shot-distance detail tiers. Paving should add non-rectangular profiles, bounded course/size/yaw/settlement variation, kerbs/gutters/borders, repair patches and wet receiver masks. Acceptance must prove the unchanged grammars in both a historic masonry street and an unrelated contemporary plaster/mixed-use street under neutral construction views and intended wet-night cameras before this benchmark consumes them again.

The rain implementation has a similarly concrete limitation. Its three depth layers are curve streak collections parented directly to the camera, so they preserve nominal depth separation but travel with the lens rather than occupying and interacting with the set. Ground splashes use a fixed rectangular world bound rather than querying visible wet support surfaces, and neither system models façade/awning occlusion, local collision, runoff, drip lines, puddle response, or light-dependent droplet visibility. This explains the overlay-like read. The next atmospheric tranche should retain the renderer-independent contract but resolve world-space precipitation against host geometry and surface queries, with camera-frustum population only as an optimisation rather than the physical frame of reference.

## Ranked subsystem decision

The evidence-saturated final ranking is:

1. production-character backend surface/identity/wardrobe/hair binding and believable deformation/performance;
2. construction-aware architectural-envelope and irregular-paving grammars with shot-scale density;
3. fail-closed character composition plus assembled-scene interaction/contact/anchor verification;
4. richer multi-scale material variation, weathering, glazing and physically coherent wetness;
5. world-space rain, runoff, splash, fog, smoke/flame and atmospheric compositing;
6. practical cue-level sound layering and final mix judgement.

No item above is accepted merely because its structural or renderer gate passes.
